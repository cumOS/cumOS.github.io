KamiOS

quiet by default.

touch the mask

An Android that stays out of the way,
until you ask it to become something else.

  • android 17
  • built on crDroid
  • open source
  • more devices soon
  1. 01

    darkmoon

    The theme engine. Everything you see is a module: fonts, colours, wallpapers, shapes, the speed of every animation. One zip, one switch. Stack them, swap them, write your own.

  2. 02

    your launcher, or ours

    Keep the Android launcher you know. Or use the custom one, written in Rust and scripted in Lua. It can be anything you want: a home screen that looks completely normal, or a tiling window manager in the spirit of Hyprland.

  3. 03

    fast

    The whole source is tuned for performance, with custom pipelines and our own blur algorithm.

  4. 04

    recovery

    The recovery joins your Wi-Fi and has an on-screen keyboard. Flash an update with a wireless adb sideload, no cable needed.

Open source, for anyone.

Public builds are not out yet. Other devices will follow.
